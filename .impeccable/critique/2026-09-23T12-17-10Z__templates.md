## Design Health Score

- 1. Visibility of system status — **3**. Flash, unread, and topic chips report state. “Enabled”, “Connected”, and “Solved” do not share one status token, so the signal changes shape by surface.
- 2. Match between system and real world — **2**. Council language is on headings. The repeated controls still say Reply, Star, and Reputation.
- 3. User control and freedom — **3**. Back links, reset, and type-to-confirm ban exist. Owner Delete in the post menu submits immediately.
- 4. Consistency and standards — **2**. The monogram, the admin shell, and topic-status chips are shared. Buttons, empties, pagers, fields, and status tokens each have a private dialect.
- 5. Error prevention — **2**. Ban types a username. Change role wears the same danger fill as Ban permanently.
- 6. Recognition rather than recall — **3**. Most actions are labeled. Compose, the post toolbar, and the inbox star are icon-only.
- 7. Flexibility and efficiency of use — **3**. The inbox advertises keys. The sweep bar and the row menu are a second path to the same jobs, in a different control.
- 8. Aesthetic and minimalist design — **2**. Screens are quiet. One-off pills, row buttons, and empty plates add a second silhouette for the same job.
- 9. Error recovery — **3**. `.field-error` sits under fields and drafts survive a failed write. Passkey and search errors use `.form-error` instead.
- 10. Help and documentation — **2**. The inbox key hint and the features legend help. Forked controls have no shared explanation of what the control is.
- **Total: 25/40 — Acceptable.** Significant consistency work before the component language feels like one product.

## Design specificity

**LLM assessment.** This is authored for this product. Parchment, Marcellus, the monogram, the gold hash, and the status left-rule are a real material language. Search says “Search the council”. A DM header says “Private counsel”. Thread participants are “In council”.

It does not hold as one component language. Four dialects sit on the same tokens:

- **Member forum.** `.btn`, `.chip`, [templates/partials/thread_row.php](templates/partials/thread_row.php), the topbar.
- **Account lapidary.** `.scribe-panel`, `.input-engraved`, `.choice-card` on profile and appearance. Security and notification fields in the same rail use plain `.input`.
- **Operator console.** [templates/admin/_console.php](templates/admin/_console.php) is one shell. Inside the pane, each area invents its own token: `.features-pill`, `.packages-pill`, `.content-board-chip`, `.role-pill`, `.state`.
- **Messages.** A private kit: `.dm-rail-btn`, `.dm-menu-item`, `.dm-newpill`, `.dm-iconbtn`, `.dm-list-empty`.

The signature row was specified as one object with a presentation axis. The inbox does not use it. It renders [templates/partials/inbox_thread_row.php](templates/partials/inbox_thread_row.php). The lexicon (counsel, commend, regard) is in the chrome and missing from the controls people repeat: the composer’s accessible name is “Reply” ([templates/partials/composer.php](templates/partials/composer.php)), the topic button says “Star”, and the member directory column is “Reputation” ([templates/admin/users.php](templates/admin/users.php)).

**Deterministic scan.** `impeccable detect --json templates` exited 0 with `[]`. That is a false clean: a directory walk does not scan `.php`. An explicit scan of all 138 template files exited 2 with **one** finding, `broken-image` at [templates/partials/monogram.php](templates/partials/monogram.php) line 9. That is a false positive: the `<img>` is inside `if ($avatarPath !== '')` and the same line sets `src`. The detector emitted no button, pill, radius, or repeated-one-off rules. On this tree it is regex-only. Its silence is not evidence of consistency.

The review and the detector do not agree, because the detector cannot see the forks. The mechanical inventory does. Across 138 templates: 316 `<button>` tags, 187 wearing `btn`, 52 wearing `linkbtn` without `btn`, 52 with some other class, 25 with no class. `btn-danger` appears **zero** times. Templates say `btn danger`, and [public/assets/app.css](public/assets/app.css) line 186 styles `.btn.danger`. `partials/empty_state` is included twice. `partials/icon` is included 107 times. `partials/monogram` is included 27 times.

**Visual overlays.** None. No reliable user-visible overlay is available.

## Overall impression

The hall is real. The furniture was rebuilt per room. A member who learns a control on the inbox relearns it on the topic, in Messages, in account settings, and again in the operator console. The single biggest opportunity is to make status, commit, and destroy one control each, and to stop copying their geometry under new names.

## What’s working

- **The monogram is one component.** [templates/partials/monogram.php](templates/partials/monogram.php) is the identity mark on the inbox row, the thread, the post, the topbar, the DM list, and the member directory. Size and gilt change. The mark does not.
- **Topic status is one vocabulary where it is used.** `chip-solved`, `chip-needs`, `chip-decision_made`, `chip-pinned`, and `chip-locked` are the same classes on both topic rows, with the words Solved, Needs answer, Decision, Pinned, and Locked.
- **The operator shell is one partial.** Dashboard, members, settings, features, and packages share the identity row, the area tier, and the “Admin mode” pill. Drift starts inside the pane.
- **Icon and flash are real primitives.** Strokes come from [templates/partials/icon.php](templates/partials/icon.php). Success and error plates come from [templates/partials/flash.php](templates/partials/flash.php). `style=` attributes in templates: 0, which keeps the strict CSP intact.

## Cognitive load

**6 of 8 checklist items fail.** That is high load. People relearn the control instead of doing the task.

- Single focus fails. Finding a topic means ignoring a second row, a second star, and a sweep bar that repeats the row menu.
- Chunking fails. “Small labelled thing” is `.chip`, `.badge`, `.pill`, `.tag`, `.features-pill`, `.packages-pill`, `.account-state-chip`, `.totp-state-pill`, `.content-board-chip`, `.role-pill`, and `.state`.
- Grouping fails. Destroy is `.btn.danger`, `.linkbtn.danger`, `.linkbtn.is-danger`, a bare `.danger`, `.content-board-action-danger`, and `.dm-rail-btn.danger`.
- Visual hierarchy fails. `.btn` is the primary fill, and it is also Upload avatar, Resend verification, OAuth, and Apply filters.
- One thing at a time passes. Each write is still one form.
- Minimal choices fails. See the decision points below.
- Working memory passes. The cost is relearning a control, not remembering a previous screen.
- Progressive disclosure fails. Post actions exist as an icon toolbar, an overflow menu, and a second disclosure. Inbox actions exist as a sweep bar and a row menu.

Decision points with more than four options, caused by duplicated controls:

- Inbox row menu ([templates/partials/inbox_thread_row.php](templates/partials/inbox_thread_row.php)): Mark read, plus Later today, Tomorrow, Monday, Next week. The sweep bar then offers Mark read, Mark unread, Star, and Snooze until Monday again.
- Post overflow ([templates/partials/post_toolbar.php](templates/partials/post_toolbar.php)): quote, accept, copy, edit, delete, warden remove, reveal, wiki, and report can share one menu, while Delete is also an immediate submit.
- DM header menu and rail: mute, details, profile, and block or leave in the menu; block, leave, and remove again on the rail, as `.dm-menu-item` versus `.dm-rail-btn`.
- Member record ([templates/admin/user_record.php](templates/admin/user_record.php)): Suspend, Ban permanently, and Change role all submit with `.btn.btn-small.danger`.

## Emotional journey

Auth is the ceremonial peak: login and register share `.auth-card` and `.input-engraved`. “Log in” is a generic `.btn`, and each OAuth provider is also `.btn`, so the filled button stops meaning “the one commit”. Passkey is the sibling that correctly steps down to `.btn-secondary`.

Compose is the right shared shell ([templates/partials/composer_shell.php](templates/partials/composer_shell.php)). The commit is not a system button. `.composer-send` is a 36px circle (`border-radius: 50%` at [public/assets/app.css](public/assets/app.css) lines 1214–1224) with an arrow and the accessible name “Reply” or “Send”. The word counsel never appears on the control that publishes counsel.

Delete has three weights. Owner Delete is `class="danger"` on a menu row: immediate submit, danger as text color. Warden remove, in the same file, is a disclosure, a reason, then `.btn.btn-small.danger`. Account deletion gets a `.danger-zone` panel.

Ban has the right reassurance: type the username. The button does not rise to meet it. “Ban permanently”, “Suspend”, and “Change role” are the same small danger fill. Changing someone’s role feels like destroying the account.

## Priority issues

### [P1] Parallel button systems

**What.** The documented set is `.btn`, `.btn-secondary`, `.btn-ghost`, and a danger variant. Production also paints `.content-board-action`, `.packages-rowbtn`, `.features-rowbtn`, `.post-toolbar-button`, the inbox sweep button, `.dm-rail-btn`, `.dm-menu-item`, `.star-btn`, and `.board-mute-toggle`. The design-system name `.btn-danger` is unused. Templates say `btn danger`.

**Why it matters.** Commit, quiet action, and destroy do not look like the same control. A member who learns “filled button commits” on Save profile meets a circle on send, a pill on Star, a 4px chip on the inbox sweep, and a borderless label on a board’s Delete.

**Fix.** Keep `.btn`, `.btn-secondary`, `.btn-ghost`, and `.btn.danger` as the only button classes. Map row actions to `.btn-ghost` or `.linkbtn`. Reserve `.btn.danger` for irreversible commits. Make `.composer-send` a `.btn` at the 7px radius; if it stays icon-only, put the visible word on the control when the dock is expanded. Leave the documented pill exceptions (`.star-btn`, `.topic-tools-open`, `.board-mute-toggle`, `.dm-newpill`) on the open ADR 0034 question. Do not add another exception while that ruling is open. `.post-toolbar-button` is already another pill button (`border-radius: var(--radius-pill)`) sitting beside that open question.

**Suggested command.** `/impeccable distill` on the button families in `templates/` and the matching rules in `public/assets/app.css`.

### [P1] Status tokens were copied instead of extended

**What.** The shared set is `.chip`, `.badge`, `.pill`, and `.tag`. Operator and account surfaces then invent copies. `.features-pill` ([public/assets/app.css](public/assets/app.css) line 13166) and `.packages-pill` (line 13492) are the same rule twice: inline-block, `1px 9px`, pill radius, 0.66rem. Beside them: `.content-board-chip`, `.account-state-chip`, `.totp-state-pill`, `.role-pill`, `.state`, and `.pill-danger`.

The copies were reasoned. A comment above `.features-pill` says title-case labels made the lowercase `.state` vocabulary the wrong primitive. A comment above `.pill-danger` says `.pill-admin` is an accent-filled chip with three meanings across many call sites, so it was not recolored. Those are real defects in the shared tokens. The response was a new class per area.

`app.css` also defines `.pill`, `.badge`, and `.tag` twice (around lines 83–147, then again at 1525–1564). The later block is the Imladris register. The early block is a second register waiting for a more specific rule.

**Why it matters.** “Solved”, “Enabled”, “compatible”, and “Connected” are the same kind of fact. They do not look related, so color stops meaning the status ledger and starts meaning whichever area the person is in.

**Fix.** Repair the shared token so the forks are unnecessary: a title-case status chip with the existing semantic pairs (`--surface-done` / `--on-done`, review, sunken, danger), and stop using `.pill-admin` as a general status fill. Then delete `.features-pill` and `.packages-pill` by pointing those templates at the shared chip. Board and meta labels stay `.tag`. Filter tabs stay `.pill`. Do not invent another `*-pill`.

**Suggested command.** `/impeccable distill` on status chips, then `/impeccable colorize` only if the shared tones are short a danger or off state.

### [P1] Two topic rows, and three stars

**What.** [templates/partials/thread_row.php](templates/partials/thread_row.php) is the board, search, and tag row. The inbox ignores it. The star is three controls: a pill `.star-btn` labeled “Star” on the topic, an icon-only inbox button using `star` / `star-filled` ([templates/partials/inbox_thread_row.php](templates/partials/inbox_thread_row.php) line 54), and a literal `★` on the board row ([templates/partials/thread_row.php](templates/partials/thread_row.php) lines 124–126).

**Why it matters.** “Find a topic and mark it” is the primary loop. The inbox, the board, and the topic each teach a different star. The design system already said this row is one object.

**Fix.** One partial, with the presentation axis the thread-row spec already describes. Inbox selection, star, and snooze are slots on that row. One esteem glyph (the commend-star icon) and one label. Prefer “Commend” where the lexicon is binding. Retire the `★` character.

**Suggested command.** `/impeccable layout` for the row structure, with `/impeccable clarify` for the Star / Commend label.

### [P2] Empty states and pagers were reinvented

**What.** [templates/partials/empty_state.php](templates/partials/empty_state.php) (heading, sentence, optional action) is used only by [templates/admin/audit.php](templates/admin/audit.php) and [templates/admin/email.php](templates/admin/email.php). Everywhere else is a private plate: `.inbox-empty-state`, `.muted.empty` (11 files), `.packages-empty`, `.dm-list-empty`, `.member-directory-empty`, `.features-empty`, `.living-brief-empty`, and more. About 53 files draw an empty without that partial.

Pagination is the same story. [templates/partials/pagination.php](templates/partials/pagination.php) is a numbered strip (“‹ Prev”) for board, inbox, thread, and tags. [templates/partials/pager.php](templates/partials/pager.php) is Previous / Page X of Y / Next, used by audit and email. [templates/admin/users.php](templates/admin/users.php) lines 208–218 hand-copy the pager. [templates/mod/reports.php](templates/mod/reports.php) and [templates/feed.php](templates/feed.php) write their own `<nav class="pager">`.

**Why it matters.** The zero-data moment is where a first-timer learns the next step. The inbox explains itself. The reports queue says “Nice and quiet” in muted text. The package catalogue says “No packages yet” as a bare paragraph. Page controls teach two navigation languages.

**Fix.** Route empties through `partials/empty_state.php`. Keep one local class only when the geometry truly differs (the inbox reading pane’s “Choose a topic”). The action, when there is one, is a `.btn` or `.btn-secondary`. Pick one pager partial and include it. The member directory is the first hand-copy to delete.

**Suggested command.** `/impeccable onboard` for empty states. `/impeccable distill` for the two pagers.

### [P2] The field register is three frames, and there is no field partial

**What.** ADR 0034’s one field is `.input`: 1.5px `--border-soft`, 7px radius, inset shadow. Account profile and auth put `.input-engraved` on top of it (21 uses, 8 files, including [templates/account/settings.php](templates/account/settings.php) and the auth screens). Security passwords and notification selects in the same settings rail are plain `.input`. The operator settings card then overrides `.input` again to a 1px border, no inset, page background ([public/assets/app.css](public/assets/app.css) lines 3624–3634), with uppercase 0.68rem labels. Every form hand-writes `<label class="field">`. `.choice-card` is a good shared choice control and lives only on [templates/account/appearance.php](templates/account/appearance.php).

**Why it matters.** Focus, error, and “this is editable” are supposed to be one sensation. A member who edits a display name and then a timezone meets two frames inside one account rail. An operator who then sets the community name meets a third.

**Fix.** One field partial that emits label, control, and the error. Auth may keep the engraved frame as the ceremonial exception the Don’ts already name as legacy. Take `.input-engraved` off the account rail so profile, password, and timezone match. Delete the `.settings-general-card .input` override so the operator settings card uses the same frame. Do not spread the lapidary register.

**Suggested command.** `/impeccable polish` on account, auth, and admin settings fields.

## Persona red flags

**Alex (power user), finding a topic and acting on it.** The inbox key hint is real. The sweep bar is a private 4px-radius control repeating Mark read, which the row menu already has, plus a single “Snooze until Monday” while the row menu offers four durations. The star toggled with `s` is an unlabeled icon on the row and a pill that says “Star” on the topic. Bulk select is this page’s hidden inputs.

**Sam (keyboard and screen reader), operating a repeated control.** Compose’s commit is a 36px circle with no visible word. The post toolbar is 28px icon buttons at `opacity: 0` until hover or focus-within, and the same actions are duplicated into a menu. The board star is the character `★` with an `aria-label` of “Starred”; the thread star is an SVG plus the word “Star”. Admin settings labels are 0.68rem uppercase in `--text-faint`, a different label register from `.field > span`.

**Jordan (first-timer), finding a topic and replying.** “New topic” in the topbar is a real `.btn` with an icon and a word. The reply control is an arrow. Search scopes say “Replies” after the heading said “the council”. Nothing on the composer says counsel.

**Operator.** The console chrome is shared. Feature flags then show status as `.features-effective`, `.features-override-pill`, and `.state` on one table, and the package catalogue repeats the idea as `.packages-pill`. On a member record, Change role is as visually dangerous as Ban permanently. The directory empty state does not use the empty partial. The general-settings field is restyled away from the field the same person just used under Account.

## Minor observations

- OAuth providers inherit the primary fill via `.btn.btn-oauth` ([templates/auth/login.php](templates/auth/login.php)), so “or sign in with” is a stack of primary buttons.
- “Mark all read” is an unclassed `<button>` styled only by `.inbox-mark-all button`, not `.btn-ghost`.
- Dashboard queue health is a bespoke `.queue-card` with its own state line, not a `.chip` on a `.card`.
- `.card` in `app.css` line 120 is what production paints. Because `app.css` is unlayered, the design-system card in `resources/imladris/components.css` does not win. `.admin-console .card` restyles it again.
- [templates/partials/living_brief_empty.php](templates/partials/living_brief_empty.php) is a second empty partial beside `empty_state.php`.
- Reports pagination uses `.btn.btn-small` for page moves, a third pager dialect.
- The early `.badge` at `app.css` line 138 is a small accent rectangle. The later `.badge, .chip` block is a bordered pill. Same class name, two geometries, cascade decides.

## Questions to consider

- The thread-row spec already says one row with a presentation axis. What does `inbox_thread_row.php` do that a slot on `thread_row.php` cannot?
- If every status fact were one chip with a tone, which of `.features-pill`, `.packages-pill`, `.account-state-chip`, `.totp-state-pill`, `.content-board-chip`, and `.role-pill` would still earn a private class?
- Should `.btn.danger` be reserved for irreversible acts, so Suspend, Change role, Mute, and Mark all read are not allowed to wear it?
- The engraved frame is legacy and supposed to shrink. Should account settings drop it so the password field, the timezone select, and the operator’s community-name field are the same control, with login as the last ceremonial use?

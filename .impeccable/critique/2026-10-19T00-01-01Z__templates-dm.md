## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The core reading room is unmistakably authored for RetroBoards' Imladris world. Scoping the cool Bruinen river wash (`--dm-ground`, `--dm-raised`, `--dm-sunken`, `--dm-active-wash` mixed from `--info` in `public/assets/app.css:7535–7559`) inside the warm parchment shell creates an immediate, quiet spatial threshold that signals privacy without theatrical chrome. De-boxing messages into grouped author "letters" (`templates/partials/dm_messages.php:5–14`, `public/assets/app.css:8176–8262`) and reserving the mallorn-gold wash strictly for the viewer's own letters (`public/assets/app.css:8250–8254`, the confirmed DM Own-Letter Gold-Wash Exception) give the stream a literary, correspondence-like rhythm rather than a generic SaaS chat widget.

Where specificity breaks down is the collision between this two-pane reading room and the shared `.forum-subheader` row (`templates/partials/dm_list.php:24–47`, `public/assets/app.css:16697–16829`). Hoisting `.dm-listpane-head` out of `.dm-listpane` into the full-width page subheader broke the spatial bond between the conversation list and its controls: on a 1440px desktop, `All`, `Unread`, and the `Search conversations` trigger float at the far right edge of the viewport above the reading column (`docs/evidence/shared-page-row-2026-10-07/visual-review/chromium/messages-1440-light-medium.png`), while on `/messages/{id}` and `/messages/new` the `Private counsel` lock eyebrow appears twice in vertical succession (`templates/partials/dm_list.php:28` and `templates/dm/show.php:29`).

**Deterministic scan**:
- **PHP template scan** (`impeccable detect --json templates/dm templates/partials/dm_*.php`): exit code `0`, **0 findings** across all 9 PHP files (clean static markup hygiene: no inline styles, no missing alt attributes, no heading level skips).
- **Rendered HTML state scan** (`impeccable detect --json .impeccable/mocks/messages/states`): exit code `2`, **142 findings** (70 warnings, 72 advisories) across 9 state files. Filtering out false positives where the generic detector flags pinned Imladris rules (`cream-palette` on parchment `#f5efe1`, `design-system-color` on runtime `color-mix()` Bruinen surfaces and white `.btn.danger` ink, and `nested-cards` / `cramped-padding` on the `.dm-shell` CSS Grid wrapper), the detector surfaced **four genuine issues that persist in production CSS**:
  1. **`undersized-ui-text` (below 11px floor)**: While `.dm-thread-eyebrow`, `.dm-tier-pill`, and `.dm-rail-sec > h3` were lifted to `var(--text-chip)` / `var(--text-eyebrow)` (11px), group member handles and presence in the Details rail (`.dm-member .m-handle` at `public/assets/app.css:8088`) and reference card metadata (`.reference-card .ref-meta` at `public/assets/app.css:8339`) still sit at `.68rem` (**10.88px**), and recipient chip monograms (`.dm-chip-mono` at `public/assets/app.css:8514`) sit at `.5rem` (**8px**).
  2. **`cramped-padding`**: Recipient chips (`.dm-chip` at `public/assets/app.css:8513`) use `padding: 2px 2px 2px 3px`, leaving text and the `24×24px` remove button flush against the pill border; similarly, `.forum-subheader .dm-listpane-filters .pill` at `@container member-page-row (max-width: 460px)` (`public/assets/app.css:16816`) sets a rigid `width: 44px; padding: 0`, forcing the 6-letter label `"Unread"` flush against both side borders (`docs/evidence/shared-page-row-2026-10-07/visual-review/chromium/messages-320-dark-large.png`).
  3. **`low-contrast` on tinted Bruinen ground**: Mixing `--info` into `--surface-page` and `--surface-sunken` darkens the daytime ground to `#e1e1d7` / `#d8deda` / `#d4d3c8`, pulling `--text-muted` (`#5c685d`) down to **4.3:1–4.4:1** (just below WCAG AA 4.5:1) on `.dm-preview`, `.dm-time`, and `.dm-thread-sub`, and pulling `.dm-search input::placeholder` (`--text-faint` on `--dm-sunken`) to **3.9:1**.
  4. **`kicker-above-heading`**: Flags the repeated `Private counsel` kicker above `h1 "Messages"` and `h1 "Bob Brooks"` / `h1 "New message"` when both headers render simultaneously.

## Overall Impression

The Messages reading room has a strong architectural backbone and a distinctive, calm visual voice, with thoughtful progressive enhancement (live 20-second short-polling, local timezone relabeling, accessible recipient chip combobox, and 422 draft preservation). Its single biggest opportunity is **healing the header and rail seams**: re-anchoring the conversation list's controls (`All`, `Unread`, `Search`) to the list pane they govern, protecting irreversible group/block actions with confirmation, and resolving the 1440px Details rail overlay squeeze so the three-pane room works as a true workspace.

## What's Working

1. **Tonal "Private Counsel" threshold and de-boxed letter runs (`public/assets/app.css:7535–7559, 8176–8262`, `templates/partials/dm_messages.php:5–45`)**: Shifting only the interior room to the cool Bruinen river wash while keeping the member topbar and board rail in warm parchment communicates privacy spatially. Grouping consecutive same-day messages by author into unboxed serif runs — with the mallorn-gold plate reserved solely for the viewer's own letters — keeps long exchanges scannable and calm.
2. **Progressive-enhancement recipient combobox and 422 draft preservation (`templates/partials/dm_compose_fields.php:27–38`, `public/assets/app.js:2519–2656`, `src/Controller/ConversationController.php:108–122, 238–243`)**: The `To` field enhances seamlessly from a comma-separated `<input name="to">` into an ARIA combobox with removable recipient chips, live `@username` suggestions filtered for eligibility, and automatic `Group title` disclosure at 2+ recipients. When server validation fails on `/messages/new`, the compose dialog, or the reply dock, the response re-renders at 422 with the user's draft intact and focus routed to the first invalid field.
3. **Calm short-poll lifecycle and timezone-aware `<time>` progression (`public/assets/app.js:2260–2514`, `templates/partials/dm_time.php:1–2`)**: Server-rendered UTC timestamps and day dividers upgrade client-side to the reader's local timezone while preserving the exact UTC instant in `title`. Incoming messages append into existing author runs without yanking scroll position when reading back, surfacing the sticky `.dm-newpill` indicator and announcing updates via `[data-dm-update-status]`.

## Priority Issues

### 1. [P1] Shared subheader detaches list filters from the list column, shows list controls on hidden-list mobile views, and duplicates the `Private counsel` eyebrow
- **What**: `templates/partials/dm_list.php:24–47` injects `.dm-listpane-head` (`Private counsel` eyebrow, `Messages` heading, `All`/`Unread` pills, and `Search conversations` menu) into `subheader_leading` inside the full-width `.forum-subheader` bar (`public/assets/app.css:16697–16829`). On desktop (`>900px`), `All`, `Unread`, and `Search` are pushed to the far right edge of the page above the conversation pane, hundreds of pixels away from the left-hand list they control. On mobile (`<=900px`) inside `/messages/{id}` or `/messages/new`, `.dm-listpane` is hidden (`display: none` at `public/assets/app.css:8598`), yet `.forum-subheader` still renders `Messages`, `All`, `Unread`, and `Search` directly above `.dm-thread-head` — exposing controls for a hidden list and stacking two headers that both print `PRIVATE COUNSEL`. Furthermore, clicking any conversation from `?q=` or `?filter=unread` drops the active filter and search query because `ConversationController::renderConversation()` (`src/Controller/ConversationController.php:204`) and `templates/dm/show.php:21` hardcode `filter => 'all'` and omit `q`.
- **Why it matters**: Fails Cognitive Load (Grouping and Visual Hierarchy) and Heuristic 4 (Consistency). Desktop users must scan across the entire reading column to filter or search the left rail; mobile users inside a conversation see two stacked header bars with unusable list filters; and anyone triaging unread or searched messages loses their list context on the first click.
- **Fix**: Keep the list's `All`/`Unread` filters and search inside `.dm-listpane` (or scope `.dm-listpane-head` inside `.forum-subheader` to the list column width on desktop and suppress list-only filter/search controls in `.forum-subheader` when `.dm-shell.reading` hides `.dm-listpane` at `<=900px`). Remove the duplicate `.dm-lock-eyebrow` when a conversation or `/messages/new` header is already showing `Private counsel`, and pass the active `filter` and `q` parameters through conversation links and `ConversationController::renderConversation()`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

### 2. [P1] Destructive actions (`Leave group`, `Remove`, `Make owner`, `Block`) fire on a single click with no confirmation, and group rail errors discard input
- **What**: In `templates/dm/show.php:62–75` (header `···` menu) and `templates/partials/dm_rail.php:69–78, 110–114, 153–158` (Details rail), `Leave group`, `Remove [member]`, `Make owner`, and `Block [member]` are bare `<button type="submit">` controls that mutate state immediately on click. Leaving a group permanently locks the member out unless re-invited; transferring ownership is irreversible; removing a member resets their `joined_after_message_id` history boundary. Meanwhile, when a group owner submits an invalid username in `Add member` or an invalid title in `Rename` (`templates/partials/dm_rail.php:89–99`), `ConversationController::runGroupAction()` (`src/Controller/ConversationController.php:449–457`) redirects with a toast instead of re-rendering 422 with `#dm-rail` open, slamming the drawer shut and wiping the typed input. Additionally, `Add member` (`dm_rail.php:91`) is a plain text input without `[data-dm-picker]` autocomplete.
- **Why it matters**: High-stakes, irreversible group and relationship actions sit 2–8px away from routine navigation (`View profile`, `Mute conversation`), creating severe slip risk (Heuristics 3, 5, and 9).
- **Fix**: Wrap `Leave group`, `Remove`, `Make owner`, and `Block` in a two-step inline `<details>` confirmation disclosure (matching the existing profile block confirmation pattern wired in `public/assets/app.js:2839–2847` and `public/assets/app.css:8029–8037`). Wire `[data-dm-picker]` (single-recipient mode) onto the group rail's `Add member` input, and update `runGroupAction()` to re-render `renderConversation(..., 422)` with the rail open and field errors/values preserved on `ValidationException`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### 3. [P2] Cramped mobile filter pills, undersized rail/chip text, and sub-AA muted contrast on the Bruinen ground
- **What**:
  1. At `@container member-page-row (max-width: 460px)` (`public/assets/app.css:16816`), `.forum-subheader .dm-listpane-filters .pill` sets `width: 44px; padding: 0`, squeezing the word `"Unread"` flush against the left and right borders of the button (`docs/evidence/shared-page-row-2026-10-07/visual-review/chromium/messages-320-dark-large.png`).
  2. `.dm-chip` (`public/assets/app.css:8513–8515`) has `padding: 2px 2px 2px 3px`, `.dm-chip-mono` is `.5rem` (`8px`), and `.dm-chip-x` is `24×24px` even on `pointer: coarse` (where `.dm-dialog-close` at `public/assets/app.css:7722` also remains `32×32px`).
  3. `.dm-member .m-handle` (`public/assets/app.css:8088`) and `.reference-card .ref-meta` (`public/assets/app.css:8339`) use `font-size: .68rem` (`10.88px`), falling below the 11px (`var(--text-chip)`) legibility floor.
  4. On the daytime Bruinen ground (`--dm-ground` `#e1e1d7` and `--dm-active-wash` `#d8deda`), `--text-muted` (`#5c685d`) drops to `4.3:1–4.4:1` (below 4.5:1 AA for `.dm-preview`, `.dm-time`, and `.dm-thread-sub`).
- **Why it matters**: Degrades legibility and touch accuracy on mobile and high-DPI displays, and leaves four deterministic detector warnings active.
- **Fix**: Change `.forum-subheader .dm-listpane-filters .pill` in `public/assets/app.css:16816` from `width: 44px; padding: 0;` to `min-width: 44px; padding-inline: var(--space-2);`. Increase `.dm-member .m-handle` and `.reference-card .ref-meta` to `var(--text-chip)` (`0.6875rem` / `11px`), give `.dm-chip` `padding: 4px 6px 4px 8px`, include `.dm-chip-x` and `.dm-dialog-close` in the `@media (pointer: coarse)` 44×44px hit-area rule (`public/assets/app.css:8622`), and define a slightly deeper `--dm-muted` token (`color-mix(in srgb, var(--text-body) 40%, var(--text-muted))`) for secondary text on `--dm-ground` / `--dm-active-wash` so contrast clears 4.5:1.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

### 4. [P2] Unresolved 1700px Details rail breakpoint forces a modal scrim overlay on 1440px desktops
- **What**: As recorded in `.impeccable/surfaces/messages.md` §8, `public/assets/app.css:8553–8564` adds `@media (min-width: 1400px) and (max-width: 1699px)` rules so that whenever the left board rail is open (`body.is-rail-open`, the default), opening `#dm-rail` on a standard 1440px desktop does not open a third column as §6 specifies — instead, it floats as a fixed overlay drawer over a scrim (`z-index: 54`), covering the right side of the conversation and trapping keyboard focus (`public/assets/app.js:2786`).
- **Why it matters**: On the primary 1440px desktop target with default chrome, a member or group owner cannot keep participant details or owner tools visible while reading or composing a message, and `localStorage` refuses to persist the open state because `railIsOverlay()` returns `true` (`public/assets/app.js:2712`).
- **Fix**: Allow `.dm-shell.has-rail.rail-open` at `>=1400px` with `body.is-rail-open` to render as a compact third column (e.g., `grid-template-columns: minmax(220px, 248px) minmax(0, 1fr) minmax(240px, 264px)`) or auto-collapse the global board rail when the conversation Details column is opened between 1400px and 1699px, eliminating the unapproved 1700px breakpoint.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

### 5. [P3] Raw event codes in Group history, raw `✦` Unicode in empty state, and per-message report form duplication
- **What**: `templates/dm/show.php:107` renders group audit events as `$e(str_replace('_', ' ', (string) $event['event_type']))` (producing raw strings like `member added @bob` or `owner transferred @carol`) with unlocalized `human_datetime()` strings at the bottom of the message stream instead of inside the Details rail. `templates/dm/index.php:9` prints a raw `✦` character (`<span class="star" aria-hidden="true">✦</span>`) with no font-size rule (`public/assets/app.css:8456`). And `templates/partials/dm_messages.php:52–65` embeds a full `<form action="/dm/{id}/report">` inside every single incoming `.dm-line` (up to 50 forms per page).
- **Why it matters**: Raw snake_case database tokens break the literary Imladris voice (Heuristic 2), and repeating 50 identical report forms bloats DOM and poll payloads.
- **Fix**: Map `event_type` values to human-readable sentence-case labels with `<time data-dm-time>` timestamps and move `Group history` into `templates/partials/dm_rail.php`; replace `✦` in `templates/dm/index.php:9` with `$this->partial('partials/icon', ['name' => 'commend-star'])`; and extract a single shared report popover/dialog or lightweight per-message trigger.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

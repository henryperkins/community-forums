Layout

The shell is factored once. layout.php renders .app-shell for every variant: app route, with a single rail partial. Routes needing a third column nest their own grid inside .main — never re-declaring the rail. All three are CSS Grid, sized from tokens. Columns below are drawn to the real 1280px proportions.

What each pane is for

one grammar, every app route

Rail · where

The community's boards, in the community's order, plus the public roster. Identical on every surface — it is chrome, not content. Never a list of items, never a set of personal scopes, never reorderable by the reader.

Centre · what

The surface's own set, and the only pane that differs between routes. Its controls follow one grammar — scope (which) · order (in what sequence) · peek (how much of each row) — and each surface uses only the axes it has.

Third · glance

Reading an item without losing your place in the list. Earned only where rows are items with state; a place gets a handoff instead. Density is never a pane control: the account owns it and every list states what it inherited.

The line is personalisation of reading. Every signal that exists only in relation to a member — unread, mentions, replies to you, watching, assigned, snoozed, starred — lives in the inbox and nowhere else. The index carries no pinning, no muting and no unread on its rows; the one personal number it shows is the rail's unread pill, which belongs to the rail and therefore reads the same on both. Search and compose are neither place nor state, so they are their own surfaces rather than panes of the index; tags, notices and connections are light enough to stay panes of it.

Where a signed-in member lands is configurable per community — the index for a forum that wants to be browsed, the inbox for one whose members work a queue — so neither surface may assume it is the first thing seen: both carry the whole topbar and state their own job in the first line under the title. Three open corrections: the inbox and DM shells escape the outer chrome by different means (a body[data-route] override vs. negative margins) — they should share one .app-shell.is-full modifier; the inbox reading pane should take the column transition Messages already has; and between the 860px collapse and ~1280px the queue’s minmax cannot yield, because the reading pane has no floor — the templates now drop the reading pane at 1280px, and the same floor is worth adding to the app.

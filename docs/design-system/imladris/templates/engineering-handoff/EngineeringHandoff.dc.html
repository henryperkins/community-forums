RetroBoards is self-hostable forum software that presents classic message-board depth through an interface people already use daily. The durable unit is the topic; the surface a member lives in is the inbox. Those two sentences are the lens for every decision in this document.

Two landings, and they are easy to confuse: / is the contents page — categories, then the boards inside each one, filtered by what the viewer may see. /inbox is the Community Inbox, the personal three-pane shell. A board directory is the way in; the inbox is where the work happens.

Three lineages combine, and naming them is the fastest way to predict how any screen should behave:

Durable topics, boards, tags, FULLTEXT search, accepted answers, moderation — canonical knowledge that outlives the session. Mentions, reactions, quoted replies, presence, and one shared rich composer — conversational warmth at the point of writing. Split-pane reading, unread and starred state, focused filters, snooze and assignment — catch up without opening everything.
The product is in Phase 5 — Gate A accepted and default-on, Gate B reserved. The closeout evidence records 1,831 tests / 9,396 assertions green across fresh and reused-schema runs. Thread Intelligence has been default-on since 2026-07-12. Three surfaces remain dark by default — link_previews, expanded_files, custom_css — and every graduated flag stays operator-reversible with features.<flag>=false. Where a screen here is mock-only, this document says so rather than implying it ships.

The shell is three panes — a channel rail, the inbox, and the conversation — and every member-facing surface is a route into that spine. The map below is the set an engineer wires first; operator and moderation consoles hang off /admin and the report queues.

Section order inside the thread is fixed: head (title, status chip, participants), the post stream with day dividers, the poll if one exists, the living brief slot, then the composer or the join-bar. Grouping breaks whenever a post gains state — accepted, OP, staff, or wiki posts always carry a full header.
Hover a post → Quote → the composer pre-fills a quote block referencing that post (parent_post_id) → Enter sends; the reply appends optimistically and the count increments. Quote is gated on can_reply, so it disappears on a locked thread. Composer → Anonymous chip → the post renders under a constant masked identity for everyone; posts.user_id still holds the real author, so the author keeps Edit and Delete. A moderator reveal is a stateless, audited action — it flashes the author once and logs it.

The textarea posts without JavaScript; the toolbar, the WYSIWYG layer, server preview, upload tray, drafts, and slash inserts are progressive enhancements over the same field. Treat anything that cannot degrade to “type Markdown and press send” as a bug in the enhancement, not a missing feature in the shell.

community_memory owns summaries, related topics, and wiki revisions; automated_context owns since-last-read context and suggested related topics. A bounded worker draws the brief (worker:thread-intelligence, scheduled at least minutely); curators publish, retire, restore, and refresh it through POST /t/{id}/summary*.

The design carries three provenance postures, and every one of them is a visible state in the UI rather than a silent failure:

Eyebrow reads “AI-generated living brief”, privacy-linked; it flips to “AI-generated · curator edited” once a curator publishes over it. A refresh that could not be checked against its sources is not published. The previous brief stands and says so; nothing is overwritten. Refresh is off for this topic; the brief stands as published until someone resumes automation.
The worker reads public posts only. Every claim in a brief cites the posts it came from, and a brief never invents a decision the thread did not make. If a surface would show a brief drawn from private material, that surface is wrong. 
-- a topic is the durable unit
threads(
  id, board_id, user_id,
  title, slug,
  is_pinned, is_locked,
  is_pending, is_deleted,
  accepted_answer_post_id,
  reply_count, view_count,  -- denormalised
  last_post_id,             -- drives unread
  last_post_user_id, last_post_at )

-- a post is one message
posts(
  id, thread_id, user_id,   -- always real
  parent_post_id,           -- quote target
  body, body_html,          -- md + sanitised
  is_op, is_anonymous,
  is_pending, is_deleted,
  edited_at, edited_by )
A thread is unread when thread.last_post_id > thread_user.last_read_post_id — no per-post read rows. Nine allowed emoji, one row per (post, user, emoji). Self-reactions are excluded from reputation in application logic, not by a constraint. Opaque tokens in a sessions table; cookies are HttpOnly · Secure · SameSite=Lax. A guest is simply a request with no valid session.
